Daily Hot List — 3–4 Best Setups, Researched
This list is not auto-generated inside the app — the app has no live market-data feed or chart-pattern scanner of its own (a fully automatic version was tried and worked in testing, but ran into a deploy-pipeline issue that made it unreliable to keep, so it's on hold). Instead I research it by hand — real earnings calendar + real quotes — and update it whenever you ask me to "refresh the hot list." Entry/target/stop are reference levels based on the data available when I last researched it — always confirm the live price on the chart before acting, and treat anything reporting earnings as gap risk: a stop-loss order does not protect you from a price gapping past it overnight.
Position Size & Risk Calculator
Enter account size, risk %, entry price, and stop-loss price to calculate.
Pure math, computed entirely in your browser — no market data dependency, so this always works. It answers one question: given where your stop is, how many shares keeps a stop-out to your intended risk %? It doesn't tell you where to set entry, stop, or target — that's your read of the chart.

Sector Heatmap — Stock-Specific or Market-Wide?
If a stock is moving and its whole sector is green (or red) too, that's a market-wide move, not a story specific to that company — different implications for how much you'd trust the signal.
